Frequently Asked Questions

What copy formats are available?

You have 4 copy options: Space Separated (default, best for YouTube descriptions), Comma Separated (for lists), Line by Line (one tag per line), and Selected Only (only the tags you've clicked to select).

How do I select or deselect hashtags?

Simply click any hashtag chip to toggle its selection. Selected tags get a red checkmark and a highlighted background. You can also use the Select All and Deselect All buttons for quick control.

How many hashtags should I use on YouTube?

YouTube allows up to 15 hashtags per video, but only the first 3 hashtags appear above your video title. Use 5–10 relevant hashtags for the best balance.

Do YouTube hashtags actually help with views?

Yes. Hashtags help YouTube's algorithm understand your video's topic, which improves recommendations and search ranking. They also appear as clickable links for extra discovery.

Are trending hashtags always best?

Not necessarily. Trending tags have huge competition, so smaller creators may get buried. Niche hashtags often work better for building a loyal audience. Our generator mixes both for the best results.

Is this YouTube hashtag generator free?

Yes — completely free with unlimited use, no sign-up, and no watermarks. Everything runs locally in your browser.
