Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a YouTube title be?

YouTube titles should be 60-70 characters maximum. Titles longer than 70 chars get truncated on mobile and search results. Keep the most important words at the beginning.

Do clickbait titles actually work?

Moderate clickbait (curiosity + value) works very well. But extreme clickbait hurts credibility. If the title promises something the video doesn't deliver, viewers leave quickly, hurting your ranking.

What makes a YouTube title go viral?

The best viral titles combine: curiosity gap, emotional trigger, specific numbers, and clear value. E.g., "I Tried X For 30 Days — Here's What Happened" combines all four.

Should I use ALL CAPS in YouTube titles?

Use 1-2 capitalized words for emphasis — not the whole title. Example: "This iPhone Trick CHANGED Everything" works. But "THIS IPHONE TRICK CHANGED EVERYTHING" looks spammy and hurts CTR.

How many titles should I test?

Generate 10-20 titles, pick your top 3, then test them using YouTube's built-in A/B testing feature (available to channels with 1,000+ subscribers). Even without A/B, changing titles after 24-48 hours can boost CTR.

Is this YouTube title generator free?

Yes — completely free with no sign-up, no daily limits, and no premium paywall.

Can I use the same title for multiple videos?

Not recommended. Duplicate titles hurt SEO and confuse viewers. Always customize your title for each video's unique angle.

Does this work for YouTube Shorts?

Yes. The same title strategies work for Shorts. For Shorts, shorter titles (under 50 chars) tend to perform better.
