Frequently Asked Questions

How does the background remover work?

This tool uses a color-based removal algorithm. It samples the background color from the corners of your image, then removes all pixels within the tolerance range you set. Edge feathering smooths the boundary for a cleaner cutout. Everything runs locally in your browser.

What kind of images work best?

Images with solid, uniform backgrounds work best — such as product photos on white, portraits on green screens, or graphics on flat colors. Complex or gradient backgrounds may need manual touch-ups in a dedicated editor.

What does "Tolerance" mean?

Tolerance controls how aggressively the tool removes colors similar to the background. A low tolerance removes only pixels very close to the exact background color (precise). A high tolerance removes a wider range of similar colors (aggressive) — useful for slightly noisy backgrounds.

What does "Edge Feathering" do?

Feathering softens the edges of the cutout by blending partially transparent pixels along the boundary. A higher value produces a smoother, more natural edge; a lower value produces a sharper, crisper edge.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your image is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server.

Is this background remover free?

Yes — completely free with no limits, no watermarks, and no sign-up required.
