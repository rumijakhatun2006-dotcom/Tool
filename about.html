Our Story

ToolMaster was created with a simple goal — to give everyone access to the everyday digital tools they need, without sign-ups, subscriptions, or complicated software. Whether you need to resize an image, convert a PDF, calculate a percentage, or download a YouTube video, we want it to be one click away.

Every tool on ToolMaster runs directly in your browser. Your files and data never leave your device — there is no server upload, no tracking, and no hidden cost. What you see is what you get: fast, private, and free.
