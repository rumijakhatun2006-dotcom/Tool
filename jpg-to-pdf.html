Frequently Asked Questions

How does the JPG to PDF converter work?

The tool uses the jsPDF library to create a PDF document in your browser. Each JPG image you upload is added as a new page in the order you arrange them, then the PDF is generated locally on your device.

Is there a working download button for the PDF?

Yes. After generating the PDF, a green preview panel appears with two links: Open (to view the PDF in a new tab) and Download (to save the PDF directly to your device). The main Download PDF button also works reliably.

Are my images safe?

Absolutely. Your images never leave your device. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. No image is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server.

Can I combine multiple JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. This tool is designed to combine multiple JPG/JPEG images into a single multi-page PDF. Each image becomes one page, and you can reorder them by dragging.

What page sizes are supported?

You can choose from "Fit to Image" (default), A4, A3, A5, or US Letter. Orientation can be auto, portrait, or landscape, and you can add margins around each image.

Is this JPG to PDF converter free?

Yes — completely free with no limits, no watermarks, and no sign-up required. All processing happens locally in your browser for maximum privacy.
