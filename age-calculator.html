Frequently Asked Questions

How is my age calculated?

The calculator uses your birth date (and optional birth time) compared against the current date and time. It accounts for leap years and varying month lengths to give you an exact result in years, months, and days.

Why should I enter my time of birth?

If you enter your birth time, the calculator can determine your age down to the exact hour and minute. If you leave it blank, the tool defaults to 12:00 PM (noon) on your birth date.

What is a life path number?

The life path number is a numerology concept derived from your birth date. It's calculated by reducing the digits of your full birth date to a single digit (or master numbers 11, 22, 33) and is said to reveal your life's purpose.

How accurate is the next birthday countdown?

The countdown is calculated in real time based on your birth date and the current date. It accounts for leap years and correctly handles birthdays falling on February 29 in non-leap years (treated as March 1).

Is my data stored or shared?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your birth date and time are never sent to any server, stored, or shared.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes — completely free with no limits, no watermarks, and no sign-up required. You can use it as many times as you like.
