Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes are supported?

We support the most common passport and visa sizes: US Passport (2×2 in), UK/EU/Australia/Japan/Pakistan (35×45 mm), India (51×51 mm), Bangladesh (45×35 mm), China Visa (33×48 mm), Canada (50×70 mm), and you can also enter a custom size in millimeters.

Can I change the background color?

Yes! You can pick any background color using the color picker or choose from 8 quick presets including white, light blue, light gray, and more. Simply click a swatch or use the color input.

How does the photo get cropped?

The tool automatically scales your uploaded photo to fill the selected passport size and centers it. You can adjust the zoom slider to fine-tune the framing — a higher zoom means a tighter crop on the face.

What does the print sheet do?

The print sheet arranges multiple copies of your passport photo on a standard paper size (4×6 inch, A4, or US Letter) so you can print them at home or a photo lab and cut them out.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your photo is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server.

Is this passport photo maker free?

Yes — completely free with no limits, no watermarks, and no sign-up required.
