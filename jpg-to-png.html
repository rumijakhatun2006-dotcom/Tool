Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG supports lossless compression and transparency, making it ideal for graphics, logos, and images that need to be edited repeatedly without quality loss. Unlike JPG, PNG doesn't introduce compression artifacts.

Is this JPG to PNG converter free?

Yes, completely free with no limits. You can convert as many JPG images to PNG as you like.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All conversion happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy.

Will converting JPG to PNG increase file size?

Usually yes, because PNG is a lossless format. JPG is lossy and typically produces smaller files. PNG trades file size for perfect quality and transparency support.

Does converting JPG to PNG add transparency?

No. JPG doesn't support transparency, so the converted PNG will have a solid background (usually white or the original JPG background). PNG supports transparency, but we can't invent transparent areas that weren't in the original.
