Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

To find X% of Y, use the formula: (X ÷ 100) × Y. Example: 15% of 200 = (15 ÷ 100) × 200 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100. Example: Old 100 → New 150 = ((150 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = 50% increase.

What's the difference between percentage increase and percentage difference?

Percentage increase compares an old value to a new value (directional). Percentage difference compares two values without direction (using their average as the base). Example: 80 and 120 = 40% difference, but 80 → 120 = 50% increase.

How do I find what percent X is of Y?

Use the formula: (X ÷ Y) × 100. Example: What % is 30 of 150? (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%.

Can I calculate multiple percentages at once?

Yes. Just change the values in the input fields — the result updates instantly. You can also save results to history for reference.
