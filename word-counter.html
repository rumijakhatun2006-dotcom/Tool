Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

The tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. As you type or paste text, it splits the content by whitespace to count words, counts characters including or excluding spaces, detects sentence-ending punctuation, and counts paragraph breaks. Everything is calculated locally — no data is sent anywhere.

Is my text safe and private?

Absolutely. Your text never leaves your device. There is no server, no upload, and no tracking. Once you close the browser tab, your text is gone from memory. This tool works completely offline after the page loads.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute. Speaking time uses an average of 130 words per minute. These are general estimates — actual times vary by reader and content complexity.

What counts as a "word"?

A word is any sequence of characters separated by spaces, tabs, or line breaks. Numbers and hyphenated terms count as single words. Symbols alone (like "&" or "@") are not counted as words.

Can it count characters with and without spaces?

Yes. The "Characters" stat shows the total character count including spaces. The detailed analysis panel also shows the character count excluding spaces, which is useful for platforms with specific limits.

How are sentences and paragraphs counted?

Sentences are detected by punctuation marks: a period (.), exclamation mark (!), question mark (?), or ellipsis (…) followed by a space or the end of the text. Paragraphs are detected by blank lines — one or more empty lines between blocks of text. A single block of text counts as one paragraph.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes — the tool is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts to any screen size, and the stat cards stack neatly on smaller screens.

Is this word counter free?

Yes — completely free with no limits, no watermarks, and no sign-up required. Use it as many times as you like for essays, articles, social media posts, or any other writing.
