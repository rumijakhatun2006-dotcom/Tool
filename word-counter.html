Frequently Asked Questions

How does speech-to-text work in the browser?

This tool uses the Web Speech API, a built-in browser feature that sends your audio to a cloud recognition service (Google's for Chrome/Edge, Apple's for Safari). The transcribed text is returned to your browser in real time. No audio is stored on our servers — we don't even see it.

Which browsers are supported?

The Web Speech API is best supported in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. Firefox has limited support, and some mobile browsers may not work. For best results, use Chrome on desktop or Android.

Is my voice data private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser. Audio is sent directly from your browser to the speech recognition service provided by your browser vendor (Google or Apple). We never receive, store, or have access to your audio or transcript.

Why isn't my microphone working?

Common causes: (1) You haven't granted microphone permission — check your browser's address bar for a permission prompt. (2) Another app is using the microphone. (3) Your microphone is muted in system settings. (4) You're on an unsupported browser. Try refreshing the page and granting permission when asked.

Can I use this for long dictation sessions?

Yes! Choose "Continuous" mode in the options. The tool will keep listening until you click the mic button again. Note that some browsers have a ~60-second timeout, in which case the recognition may stop — just click the mic again to continue.

How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy depends on audio quality, background noise, your accent, and the language. For best results, speak clearly at a moderate pace in a quiet environment. Chrome and Edge on desktop tend to produce the most accurate results.

Is this speech-to-text tool free?

Yes — completely free with unlimited use, no sign-up, and no watermarks. The only requirement is a supported browser and a working microphone.
