Frequently Asked Questions

How does the PDF to JPG converter work?

The tool uses PDF.js (a Mozilla library) to render each page of your PDF onto an HTML canvas. The canvas is then converted into a JPG image using the browser's built-in image encoder. Everything happens locally on your device.

Is my PDF file safe?

Absolutely. Your PDF never leaves your device. All rendering and conversion happens in your browser using JavaScript. No file is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server.

What quality and scale should I choose?

For most uses, quality 92% and scale 2.0x produce excellent results. Increase the scale to 3x or 4x for very high-resolution images, but note that file sizes will grow significantly.

Can I convert multi-page PDFs?

Yes. Every page of your PDF is converted into a separate JPG image. You can download them individually or use the "Download All" button to save every page at once.

Is there a file size limit?

The tool supports PDF files up to 50MB. Very large PDFs or those with hundreds of pages may take longer to process depending on your device's performance.

Why are my converted images large?

JPG file size depends on image dimensions, quality, and content complexity. Try lowering the quality slider or reducing the render scale to produce smaller JPG files.
