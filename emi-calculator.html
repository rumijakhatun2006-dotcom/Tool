Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI and how is it calculated?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — the fixed amount you pay to your lender every month. It's calculated using the formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1], where P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and N is the total number of monthly installments.

Which currencies are supported?

The calculator supports 150+ world currencies including USD (US Dollar), EUR (Euro), GBP (British Pound), INR (Indian Rupee), BDT (Bangladeshi Taka), JPY (Japanese Yen), CNY (Chinese Yuan), PKR (Pakistani Rupee), RUB (Russian Ruble), BRL (Brazilian Real), ZAR (South African Rand), AUD (Australian Dollar), CAD (Canadian Dollar), and many more. Simply type in the currency search box to find and select your currency.

Does the currency affect the EMI calculation?

No — the EMI calculation is the same regardless of currency. The currency selector simply changes the symbol displayed next to all monetary values, so you can work in your local currency without manual conversion.

What is an amortization schedule?

An amortization schedule is a table showing each monthly payment's breakdown between principal and interest, along with the remaining balance. In early months, most of your EMI goes toward interest; later, more goes toward principal.

How can I reduce my total interest?

You can reduce total interest by: (1) choosing a shorter tenure, (2) making prepayments toward the principal, (3) negotiating a lower interest rate, or (4) making a larger down payment.
