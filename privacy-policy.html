1 Introduction

Welcome to ToolMaster ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit rumijakhatun2006-dotcom.github.io/Tool and use any of our free online tools.

By using ToolMaster, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

Our Promise: We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to anyone. Ever.

2 No File Uploads — Everything Runs in Your Browser

This is the most important part of our privacy policy. Every file-processing tool on ToolMaster operates entirely within your web browser using modern web technologies like the Canvas API, FileReader API, and PDF.js.

  • Your files are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party server.
  • Your images, PDFs, and text are processed locally and remain 100% private.
  • No copies are stored — once you close the browser tab, everything is gone from memory.
  • You can even use most tools offline after the page has loaded once.

3 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate and improve ToolMaster. This falls into two categories:

a) Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Contact Form: If you contact us through our Contact page, we receive your name, email address, and message content.
  • Emails: If you email us directly, we retain the email conversation for support purposes.

b) Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Device & Browser Info: Browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Log Data: IP address (anonymized where possible), referring URL, pages visited, and time spent.
  • General Location: Country-level location derived from your IP address (never precise GPS location).

4 Cookies & Similar Technologies

ToolMaster uses cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons and local storage) to enhance your experience and support our advertising partners.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense and its partners to display relevant ads.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5 Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

ToolMaster is supported by advertising. We use Google AdSense to display ads on our site. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other sites across the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out via www.aboutads.info/choices.

6 Third-Party Links

ToolMaster may contain links to third-party websites, services, or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected by ToolMaster.

7 Children's Privacy

ToolMaster is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

8 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service.
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via the email below. We will respond within 30 days.
