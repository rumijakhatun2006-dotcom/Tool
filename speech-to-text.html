Frequently Asked Questions

How does "One Voice → Many Outputs" work?

As you speak, the Web Speech API transcribes your voice in real time. Instantly, our JavaScript runs 10 transformation functions on the text — plain, uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, bullets, numbered list, JSON, slug, and a word frequency list. Each output updates in its own card. You can copy or download any single format or all of them together.

Can I copy all 10 formats at once?

Yes! Click "Copy All Formats". This copies a single combined block containing all 10 outputs, each labeled with its format name so you can paste them into any document. You can also download them all as a .txt file using "Download All".

Is my voice or transcript private?

Yes. Recognition happens in your browser via the Web Speech API. Audio is sent directly from your browser to your browser vendor's recognition service (Google for Chrome/Edge, Apple for Safari). We never receive, store, or have access to your audio or transcript.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Safari offer the best support. Firefox has limited support. For the most accurate and reliable results, use Chrome on desktop or Android.

How many languages are supported?

Over 50 languages including English (US/UK/AU/IN/CA), Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Filipino, and more.

Why does it sometimes stop after a while?

Some browsers automatically stop recognition after about 60 seconds of continuous listening to save resources. When this happens, just click the mic button again to continue. Your previous transcript is preserved.

Is this tool free?

Yes — completely free with unlimited use, no sign-up, and no watermarks. The only requirements are a supported browser and a working microphone.
