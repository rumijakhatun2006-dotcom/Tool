Frequently Asked Questions

What resolutions are available?

YouTube provides multiple thumbnail resolutions: Default (120×90), Medium Quality (320×180), High Quality (480×360), Standard (640×480), Max Resolution (1280×720), SD (640×480), and Full HD / 4K (1920×1080 up to 3840×2160 for supported videos).

Why do some videos not have 4K thumbnails?

Not all YouTube videos have 4K thumbnails available. The maximum resolution depends on how the creator uploaded the video. If a higher resolution isn't available, only lower quality versions will show — but we always display what's accessible.

Is downloading YouTube thumbnails legal?

Downloading thumbnails for personal use (like previews, references, or offline viewing) is generally acceptable. However, using someone else's thumbnail for commercial purposes, re-uploading it, or claiming it as your own may violate copyright. Always respect the original creator's rights.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. This tool is 100% free, requires no sign-up, and works entirely in your browser. Just paste a YouTube URL and download.

How do I find the YouTube video URL?

Open any YouTube video in your browser and copy the URL from the address bar (e.g., https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID). You can also use the Share button → Copy link. Short URLs like youtu.be/VIDEO_ID are also supported.

Does this tool work with YouTube Shorts?

Yes! Just paste the Shorts URL and the tool will extract its thumbnail. YouTube Shorts use the same thumbnail system as regular videos.
